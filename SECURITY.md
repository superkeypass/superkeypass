# Security Policy

SuperkeyPass is a password vault. Security reports are the most valuable
contribution you can make, and we treat them that way.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue.** Use one of these private channels:

1. **GitHub private vulnerability reporting** (preferred) — the
   [*Report a vulnerability*](https://github.com/superkeypass/superkeypass/security/advisories/new)
   button on this repository's Security tab.
2. **Email** — security@superkeypass.com

Please include what you found, how to reproduce it, and the impact you think
it has. A proof of concept helps; a working exploit against someone else's
server does not — test only against an instance you run.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within **3 business days**.
- An initial assessment within **10 business days**.
- Coordinated disclosure: we agree a date with you, publish an advisory, and
  credit you unless you ask us not to.

## Scope

The project is in **early design** and has no released software yet. Reports
about the design documents (e.g. a flaw in the recovery or threat model) are
welcome now and are handled through the same channels.
